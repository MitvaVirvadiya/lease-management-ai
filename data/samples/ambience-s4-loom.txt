DEED OF LEASE
(Commercial — Retail Outlet)

THIS DEED OF LEASE is made at Gurugram on the commencement date set out in Schedule I.

BETWEEN

Ambience Projects & Infrastructure Pvt Ltd (CIN U70101HR2005PTC035442), the “Lessor”;

AND

Northstar Retail Private Limited (CIN U52100MH2014PTC258441), trading as “Loom & Last”, the “Lessee”;

AND

Northstar Holdings LLP, as Guarantor (if any).

WHEREAS the Lessor is seized of the premises described in Schedule I at Ambience Mall, and the Lessee wishes to take the same on lease for the permitted use.

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES as follows.

CLAUSE 1 — DEMISE
The Lessor demises to the Lessee Unit S-4 on Second of Ambience Mall, admeasuring 2200 sq ft carpet, situated at NH-8, DLF Phase 3, Gurugram 122002, together with the right to use common areas in common with other occupants.

CLAUSE 2 — TERM
The term is 7 years commencing 2026-09-01 and expiring 2033-08-31, unless sooner determined. Possession was (or shall be) given on 2026-08-01. Rent commences 2026-10-01.

CLAUSE 3 — LOCK-IN
The parties are locked in for 3 years, ending 2029-08-31. During lock-in the Lessee shall not determine this deed except for Lessor default.

CLAUSE 4 — RENT
Monthly base rent is ₹7,10,000 (INR), being approximately ₹323 per sq ft. Rent shall escalate by 15% every 3 years, next on 2029-09-01. The first 1 month(s) from rent commencement are free of base rent (CAM remains payable). Turnover rent of 5% of net sales applies if annual sales exceed 2.5× annual base rent.

CLAUSE 5 — CAM AND OTHER CHARGES
CAM ₹1,98,000 per month; HVAC ₹40,000; parking ₹22,000; marketing fund ₹28,000. GST is applicable on rent and CAM.

CLAUSE 6 — DEPOSITS
Interest-free security deposit of ₹42,60,000 (equal to 6 months’ base rent), plus fit-out deposit ₹4,80,000. Deposit shall not bear interest. The Lessor may draw against the deposit for unpaid dues on seven days’ notice.

CLAUSE 7 — PAYMENT
Rent and charges are payable on or before the 7th day of each month, monthly in advance, by RTGS. Late payment attracts 2% per month.


CLAUSE 12.1 — NOTICE TO QUIT
Either party shall give written notice of 90 days prior to expiration or earlier determination.


CLAUSE 9 — USE AND RADIUS
Permitted use: Home textiles. Exclusive: Home linen on Second floor. The Lessee shall not open a competing outlet within 3 km of the mall entrance.

CLAUSE 10 — OPTIONS
Renewal option: yes, 5 years, notice 180 days.
Expansion / ROFR: yes.
Early termination (post lock-in): no.

CLAUSE 11 — COMPLIANCE
Public liability insurance of not less than ₹2,20,00,000. Signage: Fascia + atrium. Assignment: Consent required. Fit-out period: 50 days.

CLAUSE 13 — NOTICES
Notices to the Lessee at: Northstar Retail, 14th Floor, One BKC, Mumbai 400051.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have executed this deed.
