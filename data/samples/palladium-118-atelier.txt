DEED OF LEASE
(Commercial — Retail Outlet)

THIS DEED OF LEASE is made at Mumbai on the commencement date set out in Schedule I.

BETWEEN

Palladium Constructions Pvt Ltd (CIN U45200MH2007PTC174221), the “Lessor”;

AND

Northstar Retail Private Limited (CIN U52100MH2014PTC258441), trading as “Northstar Atelier”, the “Lessee”;

AND

Northstar Holdings LLP, as Guarantor (if any).

WHEREAS the Lessor is seized of the premises described in Schedule I at Phoenix Palladium, and the Lessee wishes to take the same on lease for the permitted use.

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES as follows.

CLAUSE 1 — DEMISE
The Lessor demises to the Lessee Unit G-118 on Ground of Phoenix Palladium, admeasuring 3200 sq ft carpet, situated at Senapati Bapat Marg, Lower Parel, Mumbai 400013, together with the right to use common areas in common with other occupants.

CLAUSE 2 — TERM
The term is 7 years commencing 2023-09-15 and expiring 2030-09-14, unless sooner determined. Possession was (or shall be) given on 2023-08-01. Rent commences 2023-09-15.

CLAUSE 3 — LOCK-IN
The parties are locked in for 3 years, ending 2026-09-14. During lock-in the Lessee shall not determine this deed except for Lessor default.

CLAUSE 4 — RENT
Monthly base rent is ₹12,40,000 (INR), being approximately ₹388 per sq ft. Rent shall escalate by 12% every 3 years, next on 2026-09-15. The first 1 month(s) from rent commencement are free of base rent (CAM remains payable). Turnover rent of 6% of net sales applies if annual sales exceed 2.5× annual base rent.

CLAUSE 5 — CAM AND OTHER CHARGES
CAM ₹3,20,000 per month; HVAC ₹64,000; parking ₹36,000; marketing fund ₹48,000. GST is applicable on rent and CAM.

CLAUSE 6 — DEPOSITS
Interest-free security deposit of ₹74,40,000 (equal to 6 months’ base rent), plus fit-out deposit ₹9,00,000. Deposit shall not bear interest. The Lessor may draw against the deposit for unpaid dues on seven days’ notice.

CLAUSE 7 — PAYMENT
Rent and charges are payable on or before the 5th day of each month, monthly in advance, by NACH mandate. Late payment attracts 1.5% per month.


CLAUSE 12.1 — NOTICE TO QUIT
Either party shall give written notice of 120 days prior to expiration or earlier determination.


CLAUSE 9 — USE AND RADIUS
Permitted use: Premium womenswear and atelier services. Exclusive: Premium womenswear on Ground floor wing G. The Lessee shall not open a competing outlet within 2 km of the mall entrance.

CLAUSE 10 — OPTIONS
Renewal option: yes, 5 years, notice 180 days.
Expansion / ROFR: yes.
Early termination (post lock-in): no.

CLAUSE 11 — COMPLIANCE
Public liability insurance of not less than ₹5,00,00,000. Signage: Fascia + atrium blade. Assignment: Consent required; group companies permitted on notice. Fit-out period: 45 days.

CLAUSE 13 — NOTICES
Notices to the Lessee at: Northstar Retail, 14th Floor, One BKC, Mumbai 400051.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have executed this deed.
