DEED OF LEASE
(Commercial — Retail Outlet)

THIS DEED OF LEASE is made at Mohali on the commencement date set out in Schedule I.

BETWEEN

Virtuous Retail Punjab LLP (CIN AAM-4412), the “Lessor”;

AND

Northstar Retail Private Limited (CIN U52100MH2014PTC258441), trading as “Kettle & Copper”, the “Lessee”;

AND

None, as Guarantor (if any).

WHEREAS the Lessor is seized of the premises described in Schedule I at VR Punjab, and the Lessee wishes to take the same on lease for the permitted use.

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES as follows.

CLAUSE 1 — DEMISE
The Lessor demises to the Lessee Unit A-12 on Food court mezzanine of VR Punjab, admeasuring 1620 sq ft carpet, situated at Chandigarh-Delhi Highway, Mohali 160062, together with the right to use common areas in common with other occupants.

CLAUSE 2 — TERM
The term is 7 years commencing 2024-01-10 and expiring 2031-01-09, unless sooner determined. Possession was (or shall be) given on 2023-12-01. Rent commences 2024-01-10.

CLAUSE 3 — LOCK-IN
The parties are locked in for 2 years, ending 2026-01-09. During lock-in the Lessee shall not determine this deed except for Lessor default.

CLAUSE 4 — RENT
Monthly base rent is ₹2,85,000 (INR), being approximately ₹176 per sq ft. Rent shall escalate by 10% annual, next on 2027-01-10. The first 1 month(s) from rent commencement are free of base rent (CAM remains payable). Turnover rent of 7% of net sales applies if annual sales exceed 2.5× annual base rent.

CLAUSE 5 — CAM AND OTHER CHARGES
CAM ₹95,000 per month; HVAC ₹22,000; parking ₹8,000; marketing fund ₹12,000. GST is applicable on rent and CAM.

CLAUSE 6 — DEPOSITS
Interest-free security deposit of ₹8,55,000 (equal to 3 months’ base rent), plus fit-out deposit ₹1,80,000. Deposit shall not bear interest. The Lessor may draw against the deposit for unpaid dues on seven days’ notice.

CLAUSE 7 — PAYMENT
Rent and charges are payable on or before the 5th day of each month, monthly in advance, by NEFT. Late payment attracts 2.5% per month.


CLAUSE 12.1 — NOTICE TO QUIT
Either party shall give written notice of 60 days prior to expiration or earlier determination.


CLAUSE 9 — USE AND RADIUS
Permitted use: Café and bakery; no liquor. Exclusive: Third-wave coffee on food court mezzanine. The Lessee shall not open a competing outlet within 6 km of the mall entrance.

CLAUSE 10 — OPTIONS
Renewal option: yes, 3 years, notice 90 days.
Expansion / ROFR: no.
Early termination (post lock-in): yes.

CLAUSE 11 — COMPLIANCE
Public liability insurance of not less than ₹80,00,000. Signage: Fascia + food-court lightbox. Assignment: Consent required; no group carve-out. Fit-out period: 40 days.

CLAUSE 13 — NOTICES
Notices to the Lessee at: Northstar Retail, 14th Floor, One BKC, Mumbai 400051.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have executed this deed.
