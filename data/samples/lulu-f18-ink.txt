DEED OF LEASE
(Commercial — Retail Outlet)

THIS DEED OF LEASE is made at Kochi on the commencement date set out in Schedule I.

BETWEEN

Lulu International Shopping Mall Pvt Ltd (CIN U52190KL2000PTC014112), the “Lessor”;

AND

Northstar Retail Private Limited (CIN U52100MH2014PTC258441), trading as “Ink & Ivory”, the “Lessee”;

AND

Northstar Holdings LLP, as Guarantor (if any).

WHEREAS the Lessor is seized of the premises described in Schedule I at Lulu Mall, and the Lessee wishes to take the same on lease for the permitted use.

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES as follows.

CLAUSE 1 — DEMISE
The Lessor demises to the Lessee Unit F-18 on First of Lulu Mall, admeasuring 980 sq ft carpet, situated at Edappally, Kochi 682024, together with the right to use common areas in common with other occupants.

CLAUSE 2 — TERM
The term is 7 years commencing 2019-10-01 and expiring 2026-09-30, unless sooner determined. Possession was (or shall be) given on 2019-09-01. Rent commences 2019-10-01.

CLAUSE 3 — LOCK-IN
The parties are locked in for 3 years, ending 2022-09-30. During lock-in the Lessee shall not determine this deed except for Lessor default.

CLAUSE 4 — RENT
Monthly base rent is ₹1,95,000 (INR), being approximately ₹199 per sq ft. Rent shall escalate by 12% every 3 years, next on 2028-10-01. The first 0 month(s) from rent commencement are free of base rent (CAM remains payable). Turnover rent of 3% of net sales applies if annual sales exceed 2.5× annual base rent.

CLAUSE 5 — CAM AND OTHER CHARGES
CAM ₹62,000 per month; HVAC ₹14,000; parking ₹6,000; marketing fund ₹8,000. GST is applicable on rent and CAM.

CLAUSE 6 — DEPOSITS
Interest-free security deposit of ₹11,70,000 (equal to 6 months’ base rent), plus fit-out deposit ₹1,20,000. Deposit shall bear interest. The Lessor may draw against the deposit for unpaid dues on seven days’ notice.

CLAUSE 7 — PAYMENT
Rent and charges are payable on or before the 7th day of each month, monthly in advance, by NEFT. Late payment attracts 2% per month.


CLAUSE 12.1 — NOTICE TO QUIT
Either party shall give written notice of 90 days prior to expiration or earlier determination.


CLAUSE 9 — USE AND RADIUS
Permitted use: Books and stationery. Exclusive: None remaining — mall allowed a second stationer. The Lessee shall not open a competing outlet within 2 km of the mall entrance.

CLAUSE 10 — OPTIONS
Renewal option: none.
Expansion / ROFR: no.
Early termination (post lock-in): yes.

CLAUSE 11 — COMPLIANCE
Public liability insurance of not less than ₹60,00,000. Signage: Fascia only. Assignment: Consent required. Fit-out period: 30 days.

CLAUSE 13 — NOTICES
Notices to the Lessee at: Northstar Retail, 14th Floor, One BKC, Mumbai 400051.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have executed this deed.
