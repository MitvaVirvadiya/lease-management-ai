DEED OF LEASE
(Commercial — Retail Outlet)

THIS DEED OF LEASE is made at Bengaluru on the commencement date set out in Schedule I.

BETWEEN

Nexus Select Trust (CIN L70100MH2020PTC349120), the “Lessor”;

AND

Northstar Retail Private Limited (CIN U52100MH2014PTC258441), trading as “Loom & Last”, the “Lessee”;

AND

Northstar Holdings LLP, as Guarantor (if any).

WHEREAS the Lessor is seized of the premises described in Schedule I at Nexus Mall Koramangala, and the Lessee wishes to take the same on lease for the permitted use.

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES as follows.

CLAUSE 1 — DEMISE
The Lessor demises to the Lessee Unit 310 on Level 3 of Nexus Mall Koramangala, admeasuring 2050 sq ft carpet, situated at 27th Main, Koramangala 1st Block, Bengaluru 560034, together with the right to use common areas in common with other occupants.

CLAUSE 2 — TERM
The term is 7 years commencing 2023-11-01 and expiring 2030-10-31, unless sooner determined. Possession was (or shall be) given on 2023-09-18. Rent commences 2023-11-01.

CLAUSE 3 — LOCK-IN
The parties are locked in for 3 years, ending 2026-10-31. During lock-in the Lessee shall not determine this deed except for Lessor default.

CLAUSE 4 — RENT
Monthly base rent is ₹5,40,000 (INR), being approximately ₹263 per sq ft. Rent shall escalate by 15% every 3 years, next on 2026-11-01. The first 2 month(s) from rent commencement are free of base rent (CAM remains payable). Turnover rent of 5% of net sales applies if annual sales exceed 2.5× annual base rent.

CLAUSE 5 — CAM AND OTHER CHARGES
CAM ₹1,64,000 per month; HVAC ₹36,000; parking ₹16,000; marketing fund ₹20,000. GST is applicable on rent and CAM.

CLAUSE 6 — DEPOSITS
Interest-free security deposit of ₹32,40,000 (equal to 6 months’ base rent), plus fit-out deposit ₹4,00,000. Deposit shall not bear interest. The Lessor may draw against the deposit for unpaid dues on seven days’ notice.

CLAUSE 7 — PAYMENT
Rent and charges are payable on or before the 8th day of each month, monthly in advance, by NACH. Late payment attracts 2% per month.


CLAUSE 12.1 — NOTICE TO QUIT
Either party shall give written notice of 90 days prior to expiration or earlier determination.


CLAUSE 9 — USE AND RADIUS
Permitted use: Home textiles, linen, and made-to-measure drapery. Exclusive: Home linen on Level 3. The Lessee shall not open a competing outlet within 5 km of the mall entrance.

CLAUSE 10 — OPTIONS
Renewal option: yes, 5 years, notice 150 days.
Expansion / ROFR: yes.
Early termination (post lock-in): yes.

CLAUSE 11 — COMPLIANCE
Public liability insurance of not less than ₹1,80,00,000. Signage: Fascia only. Assignment: Group assignment on notice. Fit-out period: 50 days.

CLAUSE 13 — NOTICES
Notices to the Lessee at: Northstar Retail, 14th Floor, One BKC, Mumbai 400051.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have executed this deed.
