DEED OF LEASE
(Commercial — Retail Outlet)

THIS DEED OF LEASE is made at Chennai on the commencement date set out in Schedule I.

BETWEEN

Express Infrastructure Pvt Ltd (CIN U45201TN2005PTC057221), the “Lessor”;

AND

Northstar Retail Private Limited (CIN U52100MH2014PTC258441), trading as “Marrow”, the “Lessee”;

AND

Northstar Holdings LLP, as Guarantor (if any).

WHEREAS the Lessor is seized of the premises described in Schedule I at Express Avenue, and the Lessee wishes to take the same on lease for the permitted use.

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES as follows.

CLAUSE 1 — DEMISE
The Lessor demises to the Lessee Unit L5-09 on Level 5 of Express Avenue, admeasuring 1480 sq ft carpet, situated at Whites Road, Royapettah, Chennai 600014, together with the right to use common areas in common with other occupants.

CLAUSE 2 — TERM
The term is 7 years commencing 2025-06-01 and expiring 2032-05-31, unless sooner determined. Possession was (or shall be) given on 2025-04-20. Rent commences 2025-07-01.

CLAUSE 3 — LOCK-IN
The parties are locked in for 3 years, ending 2028-05-31. During lock-in the Lessee shall not determine this deed except for Lessor default.

CLAUSE 4 — RENT
Monthly base rent is ₹3,75,000 (INR), being approximately ₹253 per sq ft. Rent shall escalate by 12% every 3 years, next on 2028-06-01. The first 1 month(s) from rent commencement are free of base rent (CAM remains payable). Turnover rent of 5% of net sales applies if annual sales exceed 2.5× annual base rent.

CLAUSE 5 — CAM AND OTHER CHARGES
CAM ₹1,18,000 per month; HVAC ₹26,000; parking ₹12,000; marketing fund ₹15,000. GST is applicable on rent and CAM.

CLAUSE 6 — DEPOSITS
Interest-free security deposit of ₹22,50,000 (equal to 6 months’ base rent), plus fit-out deposit ₹2,80,000. Deposit shall not bear interest. The Lessor may draw against the deposit for unpaid dues on seven days’ notice.

CLAUSE 7 — PAYMENT
Rent and charges are payable on or before the 7th day of each month, monthly in advance, by NEFT. Late payment attracts 2% per month.


CLAUSE 12.1 — NOTICE TO QUIT
Either party shall give written notice of 90 days prior to expiration or earlier determination.


CLAUSE 9 — USE AND RADIUS
Permitted use: Contemporary menswear and tailoring studio. Exclusive: Made-to-measure menswear on Level 5. The Lessee shall not open a competing outlet within 3 km of the mall entrance.

CLAUSE 10 — OPTIONS
Renewal option: yes, 5 years, notice 180 days.
Expansion / ROFR: no.
Early termination (post lock-in): no.

CLAUSE 11 — COMPLIANCE
Public liability insurance of not less than ₹1,20,00,000. Signage: Fascia only. Assignment: Prior written consent. Fit-out period: 45 days.

CLAUSE 13 — NOTICES
Notices to the Lessee at: Northstar Retail, 14th Floor, One BKC, Mumbai 400051.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have executed this deed.
