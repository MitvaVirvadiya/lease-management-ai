DEED OF LEASE
(Commercial — Retail Outlet)

THIS DEED OF LEASE is made at Chandigarh on the commencement date set out in Schedule I.

BETWEEN

LTM Corporate Advisory Services Ltd (CIN U74140CH2007PLC030881), the “Lessor”;

AND

Northstar Retail Private Limited (CIN U52100MH2014PTC258441), trading as “Volt Athletica”, the “Lessee”;

AND

Northstar Holdings LLP, as Guarantor (if any).

WHEREAS the Lessor is seized of the premises described in Schedule I at Elante Mall, and the Lessee wishes to take the same on lease for the permitted use.

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES as follows.

CLAUSE 1 — DEMISE
The Lessor demises to the Lessee Unit G-07 on Ground of Elante Mall, admeasuring 1960 sq ft carpet, situated at Industrial Area Phase I, Chandigarh 160002, together with the right to use common areas in common with other occupants.

CLAUSE 2 — TERM
The term is 7 years commencing 2023-03-01 and expiring 2030-02-28, unless sooner determined. Possession was (or shall be) given on 2023-01-20. Rent commences 2023-03-01.

CLAUSE 3 — LOCK-IN
The parties are locked in for 3 years, ending 2026-02-28. During lock-in the Lessee shall not determine this deed except for Lessor default.

CLAUSE 4 — RENT
Monthly base rent is ₹3,20,000 (INR), being approximately ₹163 per sq ft. Rent shall escalate by 10% annual, next on 2027-03-01. The first 0 month(s) from rent commencement are free of base rent (CAM remains payable). Turnover rent of 5% of net sales applies if annual sales exceed 2.5× annual base rent.

CLAUSE 5 — CAM AND OTHER CHARGES
CAM ₹98,000 per month; HVAC ₹24,000; parking ₹10,000; marketing fund ₹14,000. GST is applicable on rent and CAM.

CLAUSE 6 — DEPOSITS
Interest-free security deposit of ₹19,20,000 (equal to 6 months’ base rent), plus fit-out deposit ₹2,50,000. Deposit shall not bear interest. The Lessor may draw against the deposit for unpaid dues on seven days’ notice.

CLAUSE 7 — PAYMENT
Rent and charges are payable on or before the 5th day of each month, monthly in advance, by NACH. Late payment attracts 2% per month.


CLAUSE 12.1 — NOTICE TO QUIT
Either party shall give written notice of 90 days prior to expiration or earlier determination.


CLAUSE 9 — USE AND RADIUS
Permitted use: Athletic apparel and community run club. Exclusive: Run-specialty athletics on Ground. The Lessee shall not open a competing outlet within 8 km of the mall entrance.

CLAUSE 10 — OPTIONS
Renewal option: yes, 3 years, notice 120 days.
Expansion / ROFR: no.
Early termination (post lock-in): yes.

CLAUSE 11 — COMPLIANCE
Public liability insurance of not less than ₹1,00,00,000. Signage: Fascia only. Assignment: Consent required. Fit-out period: 40 days.

CLAUSE 13 — NOTICES
Notices to the Lessee at: Northstar Retail, 14th Floor, One BKC, Mumbai 400051.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have executed this deed.
