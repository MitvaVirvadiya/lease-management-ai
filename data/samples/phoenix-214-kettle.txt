DEED OF LEASE
(Commercial — Retail Outlet)

THIS DEED OF LEASE is made at Bengaluru on the commencement date set out in Schedule I.

BETWEEN

The Phoenix Mills Limited (CIN L17100MH1905PLC000200), the “Lessor”;

AND

Northstar Retail Private Limited (CIN U52100MH2014PTC258441), trading as “Kettle & Copper”, the “Lessee”;

AND

Northstar Holdings LLP, as Guarantor (if any).

WHEREAS the Lessor is seized of the premises described in Schedule I at Phoenix Marketcity, and the Lessee wishes to take the same on lease for the permitted use.

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES as follows.

CLAUSE 1 — DEMISE
The Lessor demises to the Lessee Unit 214 on Level 2 of Phoenix Marketcity, admeasuring 1850 sq ft carpet, situated at Whitefield Main Road, Mahadevapura, Bengaluru 560048, together with the right to use common areas in common with other occupants.

CLAUSE 2 — TERM
The term is 7 years commencing 2019-12-01 and expiring 2026-12-31, unless sooner determined. Possession was (or shall be) given on 2019-10-15. Rent commences 2020-02-01.

CLAUSE 3 — LOCK-IN
The parties are locked in for 3 years, ending 2022-11-30. During lock-in the Lessee shall not determine this deed except for Lessor default.

CLAUSE 4 — RENT
Monthly base rent is ₹4,85,000 (INR), being approximately ₹262 per sq ft. Rent shall escalate by 15% every 3 years, next on 2028-12-01. The first 2 month(s) from rent commencement are free of base rent (CAM remains payable). Turnover rent of 8% of net sales applies if annual sales exceed 2.5× annual base rent.

CLAUSE 5 — CAM AND OTHER CHARGES
CAM ₹1,78,500 per month; HVAC ₹42,000; parking ₹18,000; marketing fund ₹22,000. GST is applicable on rent and CAM.

CLAUSE 6 — DEPOSITS
Interest-free security deposit of ₹29,10,000 (equal to 6 months’ base rent), plus fit-out deposit ₹4,50,000. Deposit shall not bear interest. The Lessor may draw against the deposit for unpaid dues on seven days’ notice.

CLAUSE 7 — PAYMENT
Rent and charges are payable on or before the 7th day of each month, monthly in advance, by NEFT / RTGS to lessor escrow. Late payment attracts 2% per month.


CLAUSE 8.2 — FIT-OUT
The Lessee shall complete the initial fit-out within sixty (60) days of possession. Delay beyond sixty (60) days attracts holding charges.

CLAUSE 12.1 — NOTICE TO QUIT
Notwithstanding the fit-out window, either party intending not to renew shall give written notice of ninety (90) days prior to the expiration date. A notice of sixty days is not sufficient.


CLAUSE 9 — USE AND RADIUS
Permitted use: Specialty coffee and all-day café; no alcohol, no nightclub. Exclusive: Specialty coffee and all-day café on Level 2. The Lessee shall not open a competing outlet within 3 km of the mall entrance.

CLAUSE 10 — OPTIONS
Renewal option: yes, 5 years, notice 180 days.
Expansion / ROFR: yes.
Early termination (post lock-in): yes.

CLAUSE 11 — COMPLIANCE
Public liability insurance of not less than ₹2,00,00,000. Signage: One fascia + one mall directory listing; no rooftop. Assignment: Prior written consent of Lessor, not to be unreasonably withheld. Fit-out period: 60 days.

CLAUSE 13 — NOTICES
Notices to the Lessee at: Northstar Retail, 14th Floor, One BKC, Bandra Kurla Complex, Mumbai 400051.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have executed this deed.
